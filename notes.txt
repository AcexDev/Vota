Setting up Google Auth

-Create users app
-Create Project on Google Cloud Console
-Define a variable SITE_ID and set it to 1
-Add 'django.contrib.sites' and 'allauth', and the few other apps to installed apps in settings
-Configure SOCIAL_ACCOUNT_PROVIDERS in settings Social Applications
-At the bottom of settings, configure Auth Backends
-Set Login and Logout Redirect URLs
-Add required urls to base app urls.py
-Add allauth.account.middleware.AccountMiddleware' to middleware after contrib.Authentication
-Make migrations and migrate

To access extracted date from Google in the shell:
-from allauth.socialaccount.models import SocialAccount
